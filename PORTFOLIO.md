## Problem & Goals

**Problem:** Hiring managers land on GitHub before company pages. An empty or outdated profile under-sells live demos and private production work.

**Goals:**

1. Clear intro: founder, Greer SC, end-to-end builder  
2. Explicit **private repo access on request** policy  
3. Featured projects with **live demo links** + stack chips  
4. Point people to company project pages and product case studies
